How It Works

Our Process

Four steps from stock to beast. Transparent, professional, and built around your goals.

01

Consultation & Assessment

We start with your goals. Daily driver efficiency? Track day dominance? Towing torque? We assess your vehicle's current health, hardware mods, and ECU version to build the right strategy.

02

Data Reading & Backup

We read your ECU's original file via OBD or bench mode — never opening the ECU unless necessary. Every original file is cryptographically hashed and stored securely. You always get your stock file back.

03

Map Development & Testing

Our engineers modify ignition timing, fuel maps, boost pressure, torque limits, and throttle response based on your hardware and goals. Each map is validated against thermodynamic safety margins.

04

Delivery & Validation

The tuned file is written to your ECU. We perform live data logging, check for fault codes, and validate AFR, boost, and EGT readings. Dyno validation available on request.
